# Fix AI Reformat Chunk Size

## Problem
The AI reformat works for short documents (500-1000 words) but fails for longer ones (4104 words). The root cause: `MAX_CHUNK_SIZE = 80,000` characters means documents under 80K chars are sent as a single request. The 4104-word document (~25K chars) isn't being chunked at all, and `qwen2.5-coder:1.5b` (1.5B params) can't handle it without summarizing.

## Solution
Lower `MAX_CHUNK_SIZE` to match what the model actually handles well: ~500-1000 words (~3000-6000 characters). This forces chunking earlier, giving the model smaller pieces to work with.

## Changes

### File: `app/services/reformat_service.rb`
1. Change `MAX_CHUNK_SIZE` from `80_000` to `5_000` (characters)
2. This means ~750-1000 words per chunk, which matches the user's working threshold
3. Add logging to show chunk count when multiple chunks are processed

### Why 5000 characters?
- 500 words ≈ 3000 chars → works
- 1000 words ≈ 6000 chars → works
- 4104 words ≈ 25000 chars → fails
- Setting to 5000 forces chunking at ~750 words, well within the working range
